   - 8-character invitation codes
   - Expiration tracking (7 days default)
   - Status: pending/accepted/declined/expired

   - Comprehensive activity tracking
   - 17 different activity types

4. family_usage_analytics
   - Monthly usage aggregation per member
   - Playbooks, devotionals, journal entries
   - Active days tracking
   - Period-based queries

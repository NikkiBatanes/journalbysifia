What is the best decision you’ve made in your life?

Some decisions are simple. Some are significant. Some change the direction of our lives.

The Gospel speaks to a decision with eternal significance.

Truth 1

God loves you and desires that you…

His desire for you includes both:

Have eternal life with Him

For God loved the world so much that he gave his only Son, so that everyone who believes in him may not perish but have eternal life.

John 3:16

Have an abundant and meaningful life with Him

Jesus said He came that we might have life in all its fullness.

John 10:10

Truth 2

We have a sin problem that separates us from God.

“Everyone has sinned and is far away from God’s saving presence.”

Romans 3:23

Our problem

The penalty of sin is death.

“For sin pays its wage—death;”

Romans 6:23a

The consequence

The Bible talks about different kinds of death.

Physical death

Everyone must die once, and after that be judged by God.

Hebrews 9:27

Spiritual death — eternal separation from God

But cowards, traitors, perverts, murderers, the immoral, those who practice magic, those who worship idols, and all liars—the place for them is the lake burning with fire and sulfur, which is the second death.

Revelation 21:8

Our efforts

Can we make ourselves right with God?

Religion, good works, and good morals cannot pay the penalty for sin or erase our guilt.

1
Religion

Religious activity cannot become our salvation.

2
Good works

Our good deeds cannot erase our guilt.

3
Good morals

Trying to be moral cannot make us right with God.

Truth 3

Jesus Christ is God’s only way to eternal life.

He completely paid the penalty for our sins.

“For Christ died for sins once and for all, a good man on behalf of sinners, in order to lead you to God.”

1 Peter 3:18

He is the only way.

In John 14:6, Jesus answered him, “I am the way, the truth, and the life; no one goes to the Father except by me.”

John 14:6

Jesus rose from the dead.

His resurrection proves that He is the Son of God, the Messiah, the only Savior.

It is about his Son, our Lord Jesus Christ: as to his humanity, he was born a descendant of David; as to his divine holiness, he was shown with great power to be the Son of God by being raised from death.

Romans 1:3–4

Truth 4

We must place our faith in Jesus Christ to save us.

Merely knowing what Christ has done is not enough.

“For it is by God’s grace that you have been saved through faith. It is not the result of your own efforts, but God’s gift.”

Ephesians 2:8–9

We are saved by God’s grace through faith in Jesus Christ alone.

Think about it

Which one is true?

Good works are not the means to salvation. They are evidence of a life changed by faith.

Responding

What does trusting Jesus look like?

1
Turn from sin

Be willing to turn away from sin.

2
Trust Jesus

Trust Him for your forgiveness.

3
Receive His gift

Receive God’s free gift of eternal life in Christ.

God’s promise

You can know today that you have eternal life.

When you trust in Jesus.

The testimony is this: God has given us eternal life, and this life has its source in his Son. Whoever has the Son has this life; whoever does not have the Son of God does not have life.

1 John 5:11–12

This is personal

This will determine your eternal destiny.

Are you willing to trust and follow Jesus as your Lord and Savior?

Pray this in faith

Talk to Jesus.

Prayer doesn’t earn salvation. It can express the faith, repentance, and surrender of your heart.

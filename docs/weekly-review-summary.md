# Weekly review summary

The editor's closing page and completed weekly reviews use `src/components/reviews/WeeklyReviewSummary.tsx`. The initial tab is Looking Back. Each tab keeps the review's original period visible; Looking Ahead also displays the following seven calendar days.

The large hero date follows the selected tab: Looking Back shows the reviewed week and Looking Ahead shows the following seven days. Looking Ahead's hero frames that period with “Entrust the week ahead to God.” This prominent header uses the full month name, such as September 14–20. Compact date ranges elsewhere and the completion date use short month names, including Sep. Dates in the current local calendar year omit the year; older or future dates retain it, and ranges crossing New Year retain both years for clarity.

Looking Back opens with “There was meaning here.” and the explicitly Christian posture “Looking back with God at what you felt, noticed, and chose to remember.” This leaves room for difficult, unresolved, or spiritually dry weeks without forcing a positive interpretation. It includes saved feeling words, a tappable daily activity chart, an expandable activity breakdown, all eight life-area ratings, bookmarks, additional memories, the hard parts, gratitude, God's faithfulness, learning, and older prayer answers. Looking Ahead opens with “Make room for what matters.” followed by “With God, consider what to carry, care for, and pray over.” The upcoming compact date appears on its own line. Looking Ahead includes priorities, care areas and notes, challenges, the Looking Forward emotion and writing, prayer, and older people/rest/faithful-step answers. Selected care areas reuse their catalog icons—such as the head-heart icon for Mind and wallet for Finances—and custom Other text keeps the dots icon.

## Data and counting

`src/services/weeklyReviewSummary.ts` builds the analytics without writing data. Counts use capture items whose `selectedDate` falls inside the saved review period. Identical presentation/ID/date records are counted once. A day counts as active here only when at least one captured moment is present, so the interface calls these "days with moments" rather than completed routines. Categories partition captured moments; they are not a productivity or spiritual score.

Life-area statuses are the user's saved answers. Missing ratings display a dash and are excluded from the rating breakdown. `struggling` is presented as `Needs care`. Bookmark resolution follows canonical IDs for older prayer references and reports unavailable originals without deleting references. A failed capture load leaves written answers readable and does not display zero activity as if the week were empty.

Saved prayer snapshots use `WeeklyReviewPrayerSummary`: Answers & changes, Brought to God, and Still carrying group the recorded event types, with unknown older types preserved under More from prayer. Each group previews its two newest events and can reveal the rest. Counts describe saved prayer moments, not unique prayers or a measure of spiritual activity. Multiple answers or needs for the same prayer remain separate. New entries show their canonical source type—such as Open Prayer, CAST Prayer, Prayer for Someone, or Bible Study Prayer—instead of the ambiguous “New prayer” label; Thanksgiving appears as “A prayer of thanks.” Future snapshots retain the optional source type, while older snapshots fall back to Prayer or A prayer of thanks without changing saved content. Answers and updates remain action labels because they may belong to prayers from earlier weeks. Short event labels and dates lead each entry; long notes expand with haptics, and identical title/body text appears only once. Still-carrying dates say “As of” because they describe the end-of-week state. The A prayer of thanks and Looking forward to this week cards use the same warm peach icon background as their card family. This is a read-only presentation of the existing snapshot.

## Interaction and persistence

The weekly review header and tabs do not use blur or glass. The navigation row uses the same solid paper color as the page, and its close glyph sits in a 34-point sage circle within a 44-point touch target. A solid sage track groups Looking Back and Looking Ahead at both resting positions: beneath the hero and docked in the header. It fades out while the controls travel so a large panel does not move across the page. The selected tab button always has the darker green pill. One set of tab controls follows the scroll continuously from below the hero into the top navigation row, between the back and close/status controls. As it approaches, the tab group narrows, its visible height changes from 46 to 36 points, the icons disappear, and “Your weekly review” fades out to make room. Four-point vertical hit slop preserves a 44-point target around each compact tab. Switching tabs while docked keeps them in the header and positions the selected tab's content beneath it; switching at the top leaves them in their original place. Returning to the top expands the controls, and pulling down past the top keeps their original placement. The transition follows the measured hero height and safe area, with no timed spring or additional momentum. Tapping a chart day reveals its moments; activity categories and longer writing can be expanded. These actions provide light haptic feedback. The initial render limits bookmarks to four and day previews to three, with controls to reveal the rest.

The editor controls the selected tab so editing a section returns to it. Edit buttons reuse the existing stages and autosave behavior. Looking Forward edits retain their two-step walkthrough. The final button remains visible at the bottom, disables duplicate completion while saving, and presents save failures with a retry. Merely opening or switching summary tabs never completes a draft. Completed reviews are read-only and do not show a Finish button. Other review cadences retain their existing reader and closing pages.

# Philippines Affordable Pricing Strategy

## 🇵🇭 **Market Research Findings**

### **Philippines Economic Context**
- **Daily minimum wage**: ₱695/day (~₱20,850/month) in Metro Manila
- **Popular app subscriptions**:
  - Spotify Individual: ₱169/month
  - Spotify Student: ₱85/month  
  - Netflix Basic: ~₱249/month
  - Netflix Premium: ~₱549/month

### **Previous Pricing Issues**
Our original Philippines pricing was **TOO HIGH**:
- ₱399 starter = 2.4x Spotify Individual
- ₱699 growth = 4.1x Spotify Individual
- Way above affordable range for Filipino users

## 💰 **New Affordable Philippines Pricing**

### **Revised Pricing Structure**
| Tier | Monthly | Annual | USD Equivalent |
|------|---------|--------|----------------|
| **Starter** | ₱149 | ₱1,499 | ~$2.68/month |
| **Growth** | ₱249 | ₱2,499 | ~$4.48/month |
| **Transformation** | ₱399 | ₱3,999 | ~$7.18/month |
| **Family** | ₱499 | ₱4,999 | ~$8.98/month |

### **Pricing Rationale**
- **₱149 Starter**: Competitive with Spotify Individual (₱169)
- **₱249 Growth**: Same as Netflix Basic tier
- **₱399 Transformation**: Below Netflix Premium
- **₱499 Family**: Affordable for families (₱100 per person for 5 users)

| Tier | Control (₱) | Variant (₱) | Strategy |

```
### 7.1 Account Deletion

To delete your account:
1. Open the siFia app
2. Go to Profile > Edit Profile
3. Tap "Delete Account"
4. Follow the 30-day grace period process

When you delete your account:
- Personal data is deleted within 30 days
- Anonymized usage data may be retained for analytics
- Backups are purged within 90 days
- Payment records retained for 7 years (legal requirement)

During the 30-day grace period, you can cancel the deletion request by contacting support@sifia.app. After 30 days, deletion is permanent and cannot be reversed.
```

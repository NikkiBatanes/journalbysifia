# SiFia Dual Market Launch: US + Philippines Strategy

## 🌍 Market-Specific Pricing Strategy

### US Market (Premium Pricing)
- **Growth**: $12.99/month ($129.99/year)
- **Transformation**: $24.99/month ($249.99/year)
- **Family**: $34.99/month ($349.99/year)

### Philippines Market (PPP Adjusted)
- **Growth**: ₱399/month (₱3,999/year) - $7.20 USD equivalent
- **Transformation**: ₱699/month (₱6,999/year) - $12.60 USD equivalent  
- **Family**: ₱999/month (₱9,999/year) - $18.00 USD equivalent

## 🎯 Fast Conversion Free Trial Strategy

### 3-Day Trial Structure
- **Duration**: 3 days (creates urgency)
- **Content**: 2 playbooks + 2 devotionals (taste of value)
- **Intelligence**: Full demo of personalization features
- **Goal**: Convert within 72 hours

### Daily Conversion Funnel
```
Day 1: Hook (Show immediate personalization)
Day 2: Build Urgency (Demonstrate intelligence, preview premium)
Day 3: Convert or Lose (Final push with discount + countdown)
```

## 🎯 Launch Timeline

### Phase 1: Pre-Launch (Week 1-2)
- **Set up A/B testing infrastructure**
- **Configure payment gateways** (Stripe for US, PayMongo for PH)
- **Localize content** and currency displays
- **Set up analytics tracking**

### Phase 2: Soft Launch (Week 3-4)
- **Launch to 100 users per market** (50 control, 50 variant)
- **Monitor conversion rates** closely
- **Gather user feedback**
- **Optimize conversion funnel**

### Phase 3: Full Launch (Week 5-8)
- **Scale to full user base**
- **Continue A/B testing**
- **Weekly performance reviews**
- **Adjust pricing if needed**

### Phase 4: Analysis & Optimization (Week 9-12)
- **Analyze A/B test results**
- **Choose winning model**
- **Implement across both markets**
- **Plan expansion to other countries**

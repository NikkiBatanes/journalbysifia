Privacy Policy

This policy explains what siFia OPC collects through the Journal by siFia pre-launch website and Early Access program, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Effective October 12, 2026Pre-launch website and Early Access
The short version

We collect the information you submit to join Early Access, a small amount of technical information needed to operate and protect the site, and any optional research answers you choose to share. We use this information to manage the list, send the emails you requested, improve Journal, and invite potential testers. We do not sell your personal information.

1. Who we are and what this covers

Journal by siFia is being developed by siFia OPC (“siFia,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), a company based in the Philippines. For the personal information described in this policy, siFia OPC is the personal information controller.

This policy applies to journal.sifia.app, the Journal by siFia Early Access list, and related pre-launch research or testing communications. It does not describe every data practice of the separate siFia app. Before Journal’s mobile app is made generally available, this policy may be updated to explain any additional information the app processes.

3. How and why we use information

We use personal information to:

  • add you to and administer the Journal Early Access list;
  • send product updates, testing opportunities, and launch news you agreed to receive;
  • understand which devices future users have and what they want from a journal;
  • select and communicate with potential testers;
  • analyze aggregated research and improve Journal’s design, positioning, and launch plans;
  • respond to questions, privacy requests, and support messages;
  • protect the site, prevent spam or misuse, troubleshoot errors, and maintain records; and
  • comply with applicable law and establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

Depending on the activity and applicable law, we rely on your consent, steps taken at your request, our legitimate interests in operating and improving the Early Access program, and compliance with legal obligations. You can withdraw marketing consent at any time.

4. Emails and your choices

Joining the list means you agree to receive Journal early-access and launch emails. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in an email or by contacting support@sifia.app. Unsubscribing stops marketing emails, but we may still reply to a request you send or send a necessary administrative message about a testing program you joined.

5. When information is shared

We may disclose information only as reasonably necessary to:

  • Service providers. SiteGround hosts the website, Supabase provides the signup API and database, and other email delivery, security, analytics, or support providers may process information for us under appropriate obligations.
  • Professional advisers. Lawyers, accountants, auditors, or insurers when needed for legitimate business or legal purposes.
  • Legal and safety purposes. Authorities or other parties when required by law, lawful process, or when reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, and the integrity of the service.
  • Business changes. A buyer, successor, or adviser in connection with a proposed or completed merger, financing, reorganization, or sale, subject to appropriate safeguards.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not currently use advertising trackers on this Early Access website. The site records page and form interactions in a local browser event queue; these events contain no email address or research answers. Campaign attribution is retained in session storage for this tab, where available. If that changes, we will update this policy and provide any notice or choice required by law.

6. Storage and international transfers

SiteGround and Supabase may process or store information in countries where their infrastructure or personnel operate, which may be outside the Philippines or your country. Those countries may have privacy rules different from the rules where you live. When personal information is transferred, we take reasonable steps to use service providers and arrangements designed to protect it consistently with applicable law.

7. How long we keep information

We keep Early Access contact information while the program is active and while you remain subscribed. We generally delete or de-identify optional product-research responses within 12 months after Journal launches or the Early Access program ends, unless we need them longer for a stated testing relationship, legal obligation, security matter, or legal claim.

If you unsubscribe, we may keep a minimal suppression record so we can honor your choice not to receive marketing. Server and security logs are kept for the period reasonably needed for security, troubleshooting, and hosting operations. Aggregated or de-identified information that no longer identifies you may be retained for product research.

8. How we protect information

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the information we process. These include access controls, restricted database credentials, input validation, and secure transport when the deployed site is accessed over HTTPS. No system can be guaranteed completely secure, so please use care when deciding what to submit.

9. Your privacy rights

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 and other applicable laws, you may have the right to be informed, access your personal information, correct inaccurate information, object to or restrict certain processing, withdraw consent, request erasure or blocking, obtain data portability where applicable, and lodge a complaint.

To make a request, email support@sifia.app with “Journal privacy request” in the subject line. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. Rights can be subject to lawful exceptions, including recordkeeping or legal-claim requirements.

You may also learn about your rights or submit a complaint to the National Privacy Commission of the Philippines.

10. Children’s privacy

The Early Access signup is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child under 13 submitted information, contact us so we can review and delete it. If you are below the age at which you may consent for yourself where you live, ask a parent or legal guardian before joining or participating in testing.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Journal develops or legal and operational requirements change. We will post the updated policy here and revise the effective date. If a change materially affects how we use information already collected, we will provide additional notice or request consent when required.

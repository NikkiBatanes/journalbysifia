FAQ

A few things you might be wondering.

Is Journal by siFia a Christian journal?

Yes. It includes Scripture, prayer, gratitude, and Bible study—but it’s still a journal for everyday life, not a devotional-content app.

Do I have to use Morning and Evening every day?

No. Use Morning, Evening, both, or neither. You can always write freely.

Does Journal by siFia replace my Bible?

No. It gives you a place for Scripture notes and reflection, not a replacement for reading the Bible.

How is Journal by siFia different from siFia?

Journal by siFia starts with writing. siFia starts with a situation and guides you through reflection and Scripture.

How much will it cost?

The core app is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. The founding price is shown above for your region. Joining Early Access is free.

Does testing include the paid app?

No. Testing is free, optional, and temporary. Not every volunteer will be selected.

Does Journal by siFia decide when a prayer is answered?

No. You decide what to record. The app simply keeps the prayer and your updates together.

When will Journal by siFia launch?

There’s no announced date yet. Join Early Access and we’ll email you when it’s ready.
